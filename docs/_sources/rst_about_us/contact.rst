Prof. Xiaofeng Li +86 (1)363 860 4484

.. _www.xiaofengli-fdem.com: https://www.xiaofengli-fdem.com/

Bio: 
 Prof. Xiaofeng Li earned his BEng from Wuhan University in 2013, and PhD degree from Institute of Rock and Soil Mechanics,
 Chinese Academy of Sciences and Monash University in 2019. He was selected for the National Natural Science Fund for Excellent
 Young Scientists Fund Program (Overseas) and the Hubei Province Innovation Talent Program. His research interests lie in the
 fields of rock dynamics and computational mechanic sciences with emphasis on the development of the hybrid continuum and
 discontinuum methods for failure modelling of geomaterials spanning the scale from micro-grain-based heterogeneity (mineral,
 texture, anisotropy, grain morphology) to macro-in-situ applications (rock faulting, geothermal-induced earthquakes).
 He is the recipient of the 2021 ROCHA runner-up of the ISRM. He is awarded as future leader of Class 2022 in ARMA. He is
 the main developer of the opensource continuum-discontinuum code (OpenFDEM, www.openfdem.com), which aims to be a free finite
 and discrete element kernel with object-oriented architecture for solving multiscale, multiphase and multiphysics (3M)
 problems that operates on various platform

Bio:
 Prof. Haibo Li is a Professor and PhD Supervisor at the Institute of Rock and Soil Mechanics, Chinese Academy of Sciences. He is a recipient of the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars of China and the National High-level Talent Program. His research focuses on rock dynamics, dynamic failure of geomaterials, and the safety evaluation and control of rock engineering under dynamic loading. He has published more than 300 papers, including over 145 SCI-indexed journal articles. His research has been widely applied to major engineering projects in nuclear power, transportation, and mining, and he received the National Science and Technology Progress Award (Second Class) as the first recipient.

Bio:
 Giovanni Grasselli is a Professor and the NSERC/Energi Simulation Industrial Research Chair in
 Fundamental Petroleum Rock Physics and Rock Mechanics at the University of Toronto. His research
 focuses on hybrid finite-discrete element (FDEM) numerical technology, experimental visualization
 techniques, and geomechanics principles applied to the study of tunneling and hydraulic fracturing.
 He received the prestigious ISRM Rocha Medal (2004) for best thesis worldwide in rock mechanics and
 supervised two Rocha Medal winners (2015 and 2017). Through the start-up company Geomechanica Inc.,
 the FDEM technology is currently commercialized and translated to engineering practice.
